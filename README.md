# AudioToText Pro 🎙️

### Privacy-First, Blazing-Fast Transcription. Your Next-Gen Android Voice Assistant.

---

## 🌟 Core Vision
**AudioToText Pro** seamlessly integrates OpenAI’s **Whisper** speech recognition technology with flexible **offline/online dual modes**. We’re committed to solving two core pain points:
1.  ☁️ **Privacy Concerns:** Refuse to upload sensitive meeting recordings to uncontrollable clouds.
2.  📶 **Network Dependence:** Achieve high-precision speech-to-text even without an internet connection.

---

## 🔥 Why Choose Us?

### 1. 🛡️ Privacy-by-Design
- **Offline Mode:** All data processing happens locally on your Android device—100% private data.
- **Online Mode:** Adopts a "zero-retention" policy. Data is deleted immediately after processing for total peace of mind.

### 2. ⚡️ Dual-Mode Engine
- **Local Speed Edition:** Optimized with **whisper.cpp**—no internet required, transcribe anytime, anywhere.
- **Cloud Precision Edition:** Powers high-performance cloud models for complex multilingual scenarios.

### 3. 🎛️ Flexible Model Control
Unlike competitors, we put the power of choice in your hands:
- **Tiny/Base Models:** Ideal for older phones—blazing fast.
- **Small/Medium Models:** Perfect for high-end devices—accuracy on par with human transcription.
